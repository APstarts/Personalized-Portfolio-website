About Me

I am a Chartered Accountant with an enduring passion for serious systems programming, AI engineering, and full-stack software development.

My engineering journey began on the audit floor. Frustrated by traditional, high-risk sampling methods, I built custom automated tools—including **3-way inventory & purchase reconcilers**, **WMS dispatch vs. stock ledger checkers**, **creditor aging pipelines**, and **TDS tax compliance validators**—enabling 100% data verification across millions in transactions.

Driven by what code and mathematics can achieve, I expanded into deep learning and natural language processing, constructing **GPT-style Transformers** and **Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) tokenizers from first principles**, as well as **private local LLM extraction pipelines with JSON schema enforcement** for earnings calls.

Today, I specialize in **systems programming in Rust**—engineering high-performance autonomous agent runtimes with dynamic planning and semantic context compaction, native desktop apps with Slint, and cross-platform web & mobile systems.

I bring a rare convergence of **rigorous financial & domain understanding** and **low-level systems & AI engineering**, crafting robust, deterministic software for mission-critical operations.
